# Privacy Policy for Hidden Object

**Effective date:** September 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Hidden Object** (the "Game") handles information when you use the Android application. The Game is published by **Muhammad Faisal Khan** ("we", "us", or "our").

## Information We Collect

The Game does not require an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, camera data, microphone data, or messages.

The Game stores gameplay preferences, audio settings, difficulty, and the local remove-ads entitlement on your device. This information is stored locally and is normally removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its app data.

## Advertising and Consent

The Game may use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as an advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, approximate location, diagnostics, and ad interactions to provide, measure, and prevent abuse in advertising.

Where required, the Game asks for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform. You may be offered choices about personalized advertising. You can also reset or limit advertising personalization in your Android device settings. Removing ads through the in-app purchase stops the Game's ad requests, but Google may continue to process information as described in its own services and policies.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google AdMob's privacy guidance](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## Google Play Billing

The Game offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to remove ads. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment card number. Google may process purchase and account information under its own policies. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Notifications

With your permission, the Game schedules a local reminder notification for 9:00 PM each day using your device's local time. The reminder is generated on your device and is not used to collect or transmit personal information. You can disable notifications at any time in Android Settings.

## Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies:

- Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform for advertising and consent.
- Google Play Billing for optional purchases.

## Children's Privacy

The Game is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can review and address the request.

## Data Security and Retention

Local settings remain on your device until you remove them, clear the Game's data, or uninstall the Game. Information handled by Google services is retained and protected according to Google's policies and settings. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## Your Choices and Rights

You can disable notifications and advertising personalization through Android settings, decline available consent choices, remove local app data, or uninstall the Game. Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights. For requests about information handled directly by us, contact us using the details below. Requests about AdMob or Google Play data should be directed to Google.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or applicable requirements change. The updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date.
